— Getting to Split

Easier to reach than you think.

By air

Split Airport (SPU) is 25 minutes by car from the clinic. Direct flights from London, Manchester, Frankfurt, Munich, Vienna, Oslo, Stockholm, Milan and Rome in season.

FREE TRANSFER · BOTH WAYS
By ferry

Overnight ferries from Ancona (IT) land 10 minutes' walk from our door. Perfect if you're combining treatment with a longer road trip through Italy and the Balkans.

10 MIN WALK TO CLINIC
By bus or car

Direct coaches from Zagreb, Munich, Vienna and Ljubljana. Split's main bus station is next to the ferry port — walking distance to the clinic.
