— About Velura

A small clinic. A deliberate one.

We opened on the Riva in 2010 with four chairs and a stubborn idea — that specialist European dentistry shouldn't feel rushed, confusing, or cold.

— 2010 → today

Our story.

Velura began in 2010 as a single-doctor practice above a bakery, two blocks from the Riva. Dr. Luka Vidović had come home after a decade in Vienna with a quiet ambition — to bring specialist-level implantology to Split, without the specialist's aloofness.

Word spread. Patients arrived first from across the Adriatic, then from across Europe. In 2021 we moved into our current clinic on Riva 7 — purpose-built with four operatories, a sterilisation suite, an in-house milling lab and large windows looking out across the harbour.

Today we are five specialists, three hygienists, two lab technicians and a coordinator — all working together on every case. Same ambition, more hands.

— Technology & in-house lab

The lab is in the next room.

Most clinics send your scans to an outside lab. We don't. Our ceramist works twenty meters from your chair. If a shade isn't quite right, we fix it in the morning, not next week.

That proximity is why Velura cases fit the first time. And why you leave with permanent teeth, not provisionals waiting for a courier.
