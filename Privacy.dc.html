Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 2026

Plain-language summary. We only collect what we need to care for you — your contact details, medical history and treatment records — and we don't share it with anyone except the people treating you.

1. Who we are

Velura Dental Clinic ("we", "us") is a fictional portfolio-concept dental clinic. For the purposes of this template, we act as the data controller for any information submitted through this website.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details — name, email, phone, country.
  • Health information — X-rays, intraoral photos, medical history you choose to share.
  • Usage data — aggregated analytics (page views, device type) with cookies consent.

3. Why we collect it

To prepare your personal treatment plan, communicate with you about your care, and improve the website. We do not use your health data for marketing.

4. Who sees it

Only the specialist team treating you and the coordinator handling your visit. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third-party marketers. For patients in the EU and UK, we process data under GDPR.

5. How long we keep it

Medical records are retained for the period required by Croatian law. Contact-only enquiries that don't become treatments are deleted after 24 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See what data we hold on you.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Delete it (subject to legal retention periods).
  • Receive a copy in a portable format.

Email hello@veluradental.example and we'll reply within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes. Material changes are notified by email to patients with active treatment plans.
